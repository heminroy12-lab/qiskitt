01 / THE QUANTUM CHALLENGE

Tomorrow's threat.
Today's decision.

Quantum computing changes the long-term security assumptions behind widely deployed public-key cryptography. Preparation starts with knowing where you rely on it.

! HARVEST NOW, DECRYPT LATER

Data collected today can become tomorrow's exposure.

An attacker may store encrypted information now and attempt to decrypt it later if sufficiently capable quantum computers become available. Long-lived sensitive data deserves priority attention.

ML-KEM is a key-encapsulation mechanism, not a direct replacement for RSA encryption. ML-DSA is a signature scheme and is a relevant migration target for schemes such as RSA signatures and ECDSA.

CLASSICAL PUBLIC-KEY

RSA-2048

256 bytes · modulus-sized public key representation

Widely deployed, but vulnerable to sufficiently capable fault-tolerant quantum attacks based on Shor's algorithm.

POST-QUANTUM KEM

ML-KEM-768

1,184 bytes · encapsulation public key

NIST-standardized key encapsulation designed to resist known attacks from both classical and quantum computers.
